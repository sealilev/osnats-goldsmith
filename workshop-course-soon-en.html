1. General

The course is taught by Osnat Levy at her home studio in Kfar Saba. Up to 2 participants per session.

2. Course structure

  • 12 lessons, once a week, on a fixed day and time set in advance.
  • Each lesson: ~2 hours. Total duration: ~3 months.
  • Tools and materials are included.

3. Trial lesson

A trial lesson is available for ₪270 (10% off). It does not commit you to the full course.

4. Postponements — student

  • Up to 3 postponements allowed, with at least 24 hours' notice.
  • Postponements due to public holidays do not count toward the limit.
  • From the 4th postponement onward: the lesson is forfeited.

5. Postponements — instructor

  • Up to 3 postponements, with at least 24 hours' notice.
  • From the 4th postponement onward: a bonus lesson is added (13 total).

6. Cancellation

No refund for mid-course cancellation. Force majeure cases will be reviewed individually.

7. Payment

  • Per lesson: ₪300 at registration (for lesson 1), then ₪300 at the start of each lesson through lesson 11. Lesson 12 is pre-paid — no payment needed. Total: ₪3,600.
  • Full course upfront: ₪3,200 before the first lesson (save ₪400).
