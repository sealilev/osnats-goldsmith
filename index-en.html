Where Every Piece Begins

A Stone With Two Sides

Omer, the manager of the kayak club where I paddle, is a man of many kayak journeys around the world. On one of them, on a remote island, he found a beautiful lava stone. In addition to its beauty, it had a perfect natural hole for hanging, exactly in the center, in just the right place. Omer gave me the stone and asked me to "just put a loop for hanging," and so I did. But something unexpected happened: the stone slipped from my hands, fell to the floor, and broke! Truly frustrating.

I told Omer and set about saving the situation. I glued it, inserted rivets, and wrapped the break with a decorative silver belt. The break actually improved the piece, which turned out beautiful. How wonderful that it broke.

Your gift, my touch, together we'll create a one-of-a-kind piece of jewelry.

A Connection from the Heart

Aviram (a fictitious name) came to me to commission a special piece of jewelry for his wife's birthday. He told me that their daughter has SLO syndrome, a condition characterized by the fusion of two toes on the foot.

Aviram asked for a pendant in the shape of a foot, with a heart where the two toes meet, inscribed with "SLO" and set with a small blue stone.

I approached the work with the sensitivity it deserved, aware that I had been entrusted with something of very deep emotional significance. Aviram expressed his satisfaction with the result.

I invite you to reach out and let me create for you the piece of jewelry that is truly yours.

The Bellows Is the Wind

Amnon has a friend who has been playing the accordion for many years. In honor of his friend's birthday, Amnon decided to commission a special gift, and came to me.

I created a pendant featuring an accordion, with the inscription "The Bellows Is the Wind" beneath it.

Would you like to give a truly unique gift to a friend? Your idea and my craft will come together to create a one-of-a-kind piece of jewelry.

Osnat Levy

I grew up in a kibbutz near the Sea of Galilee. In my childhood and youth I experimented with various crafts, and especially painting.

When the time came to choose a profession, I wanted something that also meets people, and that's how I came to goldsmithing.

I am a graduate of Wizo Canada-Haifa, and I have also attended many courses in the field.

I create based on my hobbies, crafting in ancient techniques, and according to my customers' wishes.

I warmly invite you to reach out. I'll create a piece of jewelry to your request, or you're welcome to choose from my collection.
