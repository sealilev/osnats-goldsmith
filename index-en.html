Osnat Levy

I grew up in a kibbutz near the Sea of Galilee. In my childhood and youth I experimented with various crafts, and especially painting.

When the time came to choose a profession, I wanted something that also meets people — that's how I came to goldsmithing.

I am a graduate of Wizo Canada-Haifa, and I have also attended many courses in the field.

I create based on my hobbies, crafting in ancient techniques, and according to my customers' wishes.

I warmly invite you to reach out — I'll create a piece of jewelry to your request, or you're welcome to choose from my collection.
